# Git Forge

**Dark theme** · id `git-forge` · file `git-forge.json`

> A restrained developer workspace with graphite surfaces, diff-green accents, and warm commit-orange highlights.

A restrained developer workspace: graphite surfaces, diff-green primary, commit-orange accent, and a blue hover state, borrowing its signals from the colours you already read in a diff. Geist Mono and JetBrains Mono make it the least decorative of the coding themes — closer to a tool than a skin.

## Want to try it?

Copy the contents of `git-forge.json` into StudyHub's **Custom Themes** editor — open the
Themes page, hit **Add custom theme**, paste the JSON, and save. Custom themes are
stored on your account, so they follow you to any device you sign in on, and you
get three slots to keep your favourites in.
